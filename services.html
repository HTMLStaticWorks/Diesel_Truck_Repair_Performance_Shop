OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive heavy-duty diagnostic, repair, and tuning solutions.

Engine Diagnostics

We use state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment to interface directly with your truck's onboard computers. We don't just clear codes; we find the root cause.

  • ECU Diagnostics

    Deep module scanning for Cummins, Powerstroke, and Duramax.

  • Fuel-System Diagnostics

    Injector balance rates, lift pump pressures, and rail pressure testing.

  • Electrical Diagnostics

    Pinpoint testing for shorts, grounds, and sensor failures.

Performance Tuning

Unleash the hidden power of your engine with custom calibrations tailored for towing, daily driving, or maximum track performance.

ECU Tuning

Custom map adjustments for fuel, air, and timing.

Torque Optimization

Massive low-end grunt for heavy towing applications.

Turbo Optimization

VGT adjustments for faster spool times and lower EGTs.

Calibration

TCM tuning for firmer, faster transmission shifts.
